~ Welcome to Wordle! ~

RULES
1. There is a random hidden 5 letter word stored when you start a game.
2. You must guess a 5 letter word to try and find the secret word.
3. Feedback is given as follows:
	R = The letter is not in the word at all.
	Y = The letter is in the word but not in this position.
	G = This letter is correct and in the correct position.
4. After you receive your feedback, guess another word using the previous output(s) to guide your next guess.
5. If you are correct the console will let you know, if not, keep guessing!

INSTRUCTIONS
When you are ready to start, type in the command line:
	$ python3 project-code.py
To initialize your own game of Wordle!
